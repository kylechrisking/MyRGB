MYRGB BRAND GUIDE
=================

MyRGB is a Root Labs product. It uses the Root Labs "Lab Notebook" identity (dark slate, terminal
green, JetBrains Mono and Manrope, the root@labs:~$ prompt) and adds one thing of its own: three LEDs.

See logo/png/myrgb-lockup-dark.png for the lockup.


THE MARK
--------

The Root Labs chip with its die swapped for three LED bars: red, green, blue. The middle bar is Root
green, which is how the product ties back to the lab.

  logo/myrgb-mark-{dark,light}.svg       The mark alone. "dark" is for dark backgrounds, "light" for
                                         light ones.
  logo/myrgb-wordmark-{dark,light}.svg   "MyRGB" in Manrope ExtraBold, with R, G and B in their LED
                                         colors.
  logo/myrgb-logo-{dark,light}.svg       Mark plus wordmark. The default logo.
  logo/myrgb-lockup-{dark,light}.svg     Logo plus "by root@labs:~$". Use it where Root Labs isn't
                                         already on the page: the README, the installer, release notes.
  icon/app-icon.svg                      App icon, 64 px and up.
  icon/app-icon-32.svg                   App icon, 20 to 48 px. Drops the pins so it stays sharp.
  icon/app-icon-16.svg                   App icon, 16 px. Just the three bars on the tile.
  streamdeck/glyph-*.svg                 Stream Deck glyphs, drawn in white and recolored by the
                                         build script.

PNG exports sit in a png/ folder next to their sources. Edit only the SVG sources and the fonts,
never the PNGs. Then run:

  .\tools\make-icons.ps1             rebuild everything under branding/
  .\tools\make-icons.ps1 -Install    also copy the icon, mark and fonts into the app, and the images
                                     into the Stream Deck plugin

The wordmark, logo and lockup SVGs are built by that script too. Their text is outlined from the
bundled fonts, so they look the same on machines that don't have Manrope installed.

Usage rules

  * Leave clear space around the mark of at least one LED bar's width (about 1/9 of the mark).
  * Keep the bars red, green, blue, in that order. Don't recolor the mark to match a page.
  * On a photo or a busy background, use the mark on its tile (the app icon) instead.
  * The lockup always reads "by root@labs:~$" in JetBrains Mono, with "root@labs" in Root green.
    That's the Root Labs wordmark, so don't restyle it.
  * Smallest sizes: the mark at 24 px, the logo at 96 px wide, the lockup at 200 px wide.

RgbGradient runs #FF5D6E, #FFA746, #56DB8F, #28D6DF, #42A3FD. The in-between stops keep it vivid,
because a straight red to green blend goes muddy. Use it only for things that mean "light": the
primary button, the selected-nav pipe and the welcome tour. Everything else is green or neutral. If
a whole screen is gradient, nothing on it stands out.

Presets and live device colors are the user's colors. Show them exactly as they are, with no tint or
overlay.


TYPE
----

Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows bundling them in an open-source app
as long as the license ships with them. It does: fonts/OFL-*.txt.

  Manrope           All UI text. ExtraBold for the wordmark and page titles, Bold or SemiBold for
                    headings, Regular and Medium for everything else.
  JetBrains Mono    Anything technical or literal: paths, URLs, ports, device IDs, version numbers,
                    and the root@labs:~$ prompt.

Manrope comes from github.com/googlefonts/manrope and JetBrains Mono from
github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono. Both are static TTFs, because Avalonia can't pick weights out of
a variable font.


VOICE
-----

Write like someone who built it and uses it every day.

  * Say what happens. "Corsair iCUE is running. It can override MyRGB's lighting on its devices."
  * Use short sentences and contractions: don't, isn't, you're.
  * Write in sentence case. Buttons start with a verb: "Save as new", "Open uninstaller".
  * If something's wrong, say what to do next. Don't apologize and don't blame the user.
  * Name hardware and vendor apps exactly as their makers do: "Corsair iCUE", "Logitech G HUB".

Don't

  * Use em-dashes. Use a period, a comma, a colon or parentheses instead.
  * Write hype: "stunning", "seamless", "unleash", "next-level".
  * Say "successfully". The result is the confirmation.
  * Use exclamation marks in system text.
  * Write "it's not X, it's Y" lines, neat three-part slogans, or a moral at the end.


DO AND DON'T
------------

  Do: use the lockup on the README and the installer.
  Don't: put the Root Labs chip logo next to the MyRGB mark. The mark already is the chip.

  Do: keep surfaces neutral and let the user's colors be the bright thing.
  Don't: add glows, neon or rainbow borders to the chrome.

  Do: use Root green for focus and state.
  Don't: use the RGB gradient for state, warnings or links.

  Do: use JetBrains Mono for paths and IDs.
  Don't: set body copy in mono.

  Do: re-run make-icons.ps1 after changing a source.
  Don't: edit the PNGs, or the wordmark, logo and lockup SVGs, by hand.


APPLYING THE THEME
------------------

Theme.axaml is a plain ResourceDictionary. The brushes the app already uses (RgbGradient,
RgbGradientVertical, SurfaceBrush, SurfaceRaisedBrush, HairlineBrush, MutedBrush) keep their keys,
and Fluent's SystemAccentColor values are overridden so stock controls pick up Root green. The fonts
are exposed as the BrandSans and BrandMono resources. How the app uses it:

  * MyRGB.App.csproj compiles branding/Theme.axaml in place (linked as Theme.axaml), and App.axaml
    merges avares://MyRGB/Theme.axaml into Application.Resources. Edit the theme here, not in src/.
  * tools/make-icons.ps1 -Install copies the icon, mark.png and the fonts into src/MyRGB.App/Assets.
  * App.axaml sets BrandSans on every window and popup, and OnAccentBrush on the gradient button.
  * Views use the brushes by key. Code that draws by hand (Controls/, and the status colors in
    ViewModels/) uses the same hex values as constants; keep them in step with the table above.
  * New UI: reach for a brush key from Theme.axaml. Don't add a hex color to a view.

The images in preview/ compare the app before and after the theme.
